# Licensing, Copyright, and Attribution Guide

OmniTwin is distributed under the **Apache License, Version 2.0**.
This guide outlines the legal rights, obligations, and requested academic citations for users, developers, and downstream integrators.

---

## 1. Summary of Rights Granted

Under the Apache-2.0 license:
- **Commercial Use:** You may use OmniTwin commercially in proprietary applications, internal tools, or commercial AI agents.
- **Modification:** You may modify, adapt, and extend the engine source code.
- **Distribution:** You may redistribute copies of the original or modified code.
- **Sublicensing:** You may sublicense under compatible terms.
- **Patent Grant:** Contributors provide an express grant of patent rights regarding their contributions.

---

## 2. Legal Redistribution Requirements

When redistributing OmniTwin (either in source or binary form, unmodified or modified):

1. **Include the License:** You must provide a copy of the [LICENSE](../LICENSE) with the distribution.
2. **Include the NOTICE:** You must include a readable copy of the [NOTICE](../NOTICE) file in distributions where third-party notices normally appear.
3. **State Modifications:** Any files you modify must carry prominent notices stating that you changed the files (Apache-2.0 Section 4b).
4. **Retain Copyright & Attribution:** You must retain all existing copyright, patent, trademark, and attribution notices from the source code.

---

## 3. Academic & Technical Citation Request

The maintainers request (as an academic and professional courtesy, not an extra-legal restriction) that researchers, benchmark authors, and derivative projects cite OmniTwin:

```bibtex
@software{aboammar2026omnitwin,
  author       = {Mamdouh Aboammar},
  title        = {{OmniTwin: Cognitive Continuity and Grounded Memory Engine for AI Agents}},
  year         = {2026},
  publisher    = {GitHub},
  journal      = {GitHub repository},
  howpublished = {\url{https://github.com/imMamdouhaboammar/OmniTwin}},
  version      = {0.1.0}
}
```

> **Legal Note on Citation:** Apache-2.0 requires preservation of copyright and notice files in qualifying distributions, but does not impose a mandatory branding/citation requirement in the user interface of applications using the engine. The bibtex citation requested above is voluntary community best practice.
